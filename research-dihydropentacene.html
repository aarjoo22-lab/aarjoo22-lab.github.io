02 · Master's project · IISER TVM

Dihydropentacene Photophysics & Crystallography

Synthesis and excited-state studies of dihydropentacene chromophores.

Period
Dec 2024 – Present (on hold; resuming Jan 2027)
Institution
IISER Thiruvananthapuram
Supervisor
Prof. Mahesh Hariharan
  • Set up Suzuki-Miyaura coupling, Buchwald-Hartwig cross-coupling, imidation and condensation reactions.
  • Characterised compounds using ¹H NMR, MALDI-TOF, X-ray diffraction, UV-Vis absorption, fluorimetry and TCSPC.
  • Performed DFT calculations in Gaussian for ground- and excited-state studies of dihydropentacene derivatives.
