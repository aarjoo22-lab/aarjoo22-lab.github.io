MXene-Based Electrocatalytic Devices

A NiW/MXene cathode for hydrogen evolution with a 49 mV overpotential.

Period
May 2024 – July 2024
Institution
CSIR-Central Scientific Instruments Organisation (CSIO), Chandigarh
Supervisor
Dr. Pooja Devi
  • Developed a NiW/MXene cathode for the hydrogen evolution reaction (HER) from literature review.
  • Device performance: 49 mV overpotential (among the lowest reported for non-Pt MXene systems) with 24-hour operational stability at 100 mA/cm².
  • Fabricated and tested cells using LSV, CV, EIS, chronoamperometry and chronopotentiometry with iR compensation (Autolab NOVA).
  • Demonstrated >95% methylene blue degradation in 120 minutes; kinetics monitored by UV-Vis spectroscopy.
