Magnetic Pyrochlore Materials

Sm₂Ru₂O₇ and SmLaRu₂O₇ pyrochlore oxides and their magnetism.

Period
Dec 2025 – Apr 2026
Institution
IISER Thiruvananthapuram
Supervisor
Dr. Tuhin Subhra Maity
  • Synthesised Sm₂Ru₂O₇ and SmLaRu₂O₇ via solid-state reaction; confirmed phase purity by powder XRD, XPS and Raman spectroscopy.
  • Investigated magnetism by VSM and PPMS — temperature- and field-dependent measurements.
  • Observed complex magnetic behaviour requiring further theoretical modelling; data archived for collaborative analysis.
