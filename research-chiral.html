Chiral Singlet Fission Chromophores

Harnessing chirality to control exciton dynamics in singlet fission.

Period
July 2026 – Dec 2026
Institution
École polytechnique fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL), Switzerland
Supervisor
Prof. Sascha Feldmann
  • Designing and synthesising a chiral-bridged dipyrrolonaphthyridinedione (DPND) dimer via a 5-step modified synthetic route for singlet fission (SF) applications.
  • Characterisation via ¹H NMR, UV-Vis absorption, emission, time-correlated single photon counting (TCSPC) and transient absorption spectroscopy.
